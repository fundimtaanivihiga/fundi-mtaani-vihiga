About Fundi Mtagani Vihiga

Fundi Mtagani Vihiga was created to solve a simple problem: finding a trustworthy, skilled fundi in Vihiga County was too hard. Word of mouth only goes so far, and customers were often left guessing about who to trust.

Our platform brings local tradespeople online — electricians, plumbers, welders, masons and carpenters — with verified profiles, real work samples and direct contact details. Customers can compare, choose and hire with confidence.

For fundis, the platform is a shopfront. It helps them reach more customers, showcase completed work and build a reputation that travels beyond their own village.

We are proudly local. Every profile is reviewed before publishing, and we continue to grow the platform one fundi at a time.
